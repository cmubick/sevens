How to play

The app has full instructions under How to play on the start menu, and a guided Practice round. The rules in brief:

  • Be the first to play all your cards. Cards left in your hand score against you, and the lowest score wins.
  • Whoever holds the 7 of spades opens the round.
  • Every suit builds outward from its 7: up to the king and down to the ace.
  • A card in hearts, clubs or diamonds can only be played once the same card in spades is on the table.
  • If you can play, you must. If you cannot, you ask the player before you for a card. They choose which one, and your turn ends.
  • Ace to 9 score 5 points each, 10 to king score 10 each.

Common questions

Why can't I play a card that looks like it fits?

It needs two things: the card next to it in its own suit, and the same card in spades. To play the 9 of hearts, both the 8 of hearts and the 9 of spades must already be on the table.

What is a dead card?

When jokers are switched on, a joker can stand in for any playable card. The real card it replaced is dead: it can never be played and counts 30 points for whoever holds it at the end of the round. Hand it on when another player asks you for a card.

Who do I ask for a card?

The player who played just before you. In the app that is East.

How does the game end?

A round ends when someone runs out of cards. The game ends when any player reaches the target score you chose (50, 100, 200 or 500), and the player with the lowest total wins.

What does the full game add?

A one-time purchase unlocks jokers, the Long and Epic game lengths, and the History screen. Short and Standard games are free. Tips are optional and unlock nothing extra.

I bought the full game but it is locked again.

On the start menu tap Unlock full game, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I clear my game history?

History is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it.

The phone does not vibrate.

Haptics follow your iPhone's settings: check Settings, then Sounds & Haptics, then System Haptics. iPads do not have haptics.
