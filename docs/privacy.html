Sevens

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Sevens is a card game you play on your own device against computer opponents. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. Sevens does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The app:

  • has no accounts and never asks who you are;
  • makes no network connections;
  • contains no advertising;
  • contains no analytics or tracking tools;
  • includes no third-party software that collects data.

What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Game history: for each game you finish, the date, the game length and joker setting you chose, the final scores and the number of rounds. This is what the History screen uses to show how you are doing.
  • A setting that records that you have already seen the instructions, so they are not shown again every time.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and the developer cannot see it. If you back up your device with Apple's own backup services, it may be included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase and optional tips. These are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The app never sees your name, payment details or address; it only learns from Apple whether the full game has been unlocked on your account.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app from your device deletes everything it has stored.

Children

Sevens does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what is described here, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
